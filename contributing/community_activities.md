# Community Activities

Coding for Reproducible Research (CfRR) is shaped by the people who learn from, teach with and contribute to our courses. Alongside our workshops, community activities provide opportunities to develop technical skills, share experience and improve the programme together.

This page celebrates those contributions and records what we have achieved, starting with our first Co-Development Day.

## CfRR Co-Development Days

Co-development days combine practical training with supported opportunities to contribute to the CfRR teaching materials. Participants can practise collaborative coding skills while making improvements that benefit other learners, from correcting examples and refining quizzes to developing new short courses.

The aim is to build contributors' skills and confidence, improve the course materials and grow the community behind CfRR.
